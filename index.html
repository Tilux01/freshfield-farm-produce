FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver to most major metropolitan areas across the continental US. Enter your zip code at checkout to see if we serve your neighborhood. We're expanding to new cities every month.

How fresh is the produce, really?

Most of our produce is harvested within 24 hours of delivery. Because we work directly with farms and skip the distribution center, your food spends less time in transit and arrives far fresher than grocery store alternatives.

Can I customize my subscription box?

Absolutely. You can swap items, add extras, and set preferences for your weekly box. You can also skip a week or pause your subscription anytime from your account dashboard.

What if I'm not happy with my order?

We stand behind every item we deliver. If anything arrives damaged or less than fresh, contact us within 24 hours and we'll make it right with a replacement or full refund — no questions asked.

How does the packaging work?

All our packaging is either compostable, recyclable, or reusable. Boxes are made from recycled cardboard, and insulation is made from recycled denim or compostable materials. We pick up packaging on your next delivery if you'd like.
